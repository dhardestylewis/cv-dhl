# Profile (RBC Capital Markets interview and bio)
- Head of Product and Commercialization at Bellwether, X (Alphabet).
- Joined Google 2015 in Area 120 (internal incubator); founding team of Orion Wifi (commercialization and business development), acquired by Google Devices and Services in 2022 and integrated into Android; before that, customer lifetime value modeling on Google's financial services platform.
- Earlier: founder of KarmaConnect (corporate giving platform; Vanity Fair top sustainability startups of 2013; MIT Accelerator).
- Education: BS Business Management, Stony Brook University; studied computer science and data analytics at Stony Brook and Copenhagen Business School.

# What Bellwether says it is (interview plus x.company)
- "Prediction engine for the Earth and everything on it." Earth broken into 100m by 100m pixels; state of about 600 geodata layers tracked daily (vegetation density, soil moisture, wind speed, rainfall, solar radiation, elevation and more); every structure modeled using Google Maps data (materials, window count, age, roof type, nearby energy infrastructure); tens of thousands of variables daily; building-level wildfire, hurricane and severe weather predictions; aggregable to any geography.
- Products: Bellwether Fire (wildfire prediction; x.company claims forecasts up to five years ahead), Bellwether Rapid Response (aerial image matching for post-disaster scene assessment), Bellwether Insure.
- Traction claims (company or interviewee, not independently verified): Hiscox using the wildfire models to expand California cover; a reinsurer used the models in regions it had never reinsured; Kansas City emergency management drill, damage mapped over a test neighborhood in 45 minutes at 96% match to human assessment; hurricane tool takes 50 predicted paths and gives damage likelihood up to 15 days before landfall using a Google Research model; partners include Swiss Re, U.S. Air Force, Defense Innovation Unit, National Guard, Civil Air Patrol.
- Stated ambition: risk removal through simulated mitigation, not just risk distribution.
