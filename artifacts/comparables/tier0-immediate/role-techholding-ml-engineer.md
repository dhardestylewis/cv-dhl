# Posting text, condensed

About Tech Holding: full-service consulting firm founded on delivering predictable outcomes; founders and team have held senior positions from startups to Fortune 50 firms.

Role: hands-on ML engineer for a project-based assignment building an AI-powered risk intelligence platform that helps enterprises detect, assess and respond to emerging online threats (disinformation, reputational attacks, security risks). Design, build and deploy NLP and LLM-based models over large volumes of mainstream and niche online sources. Individual contributor working with data engineers, backend engineers and product.

Responsibilities:
- Design, train and deploy models for text classification, entity extraction, clustering, narrative and topic detection.
- Build LLM features: prompt design, retrieval-augmented generation, agentic pipelines.
- Embedding and semantic-search pipelines to group content and surface emerging threats.
- Productionize models as monitored services with latency, cost and accuracy targets.
- Evaluation frameworks and datasets for quality, bias and drift.
- Improve ingestion, labeling and feature pipelines for high-volume, noisy, multilingual text.
- Monitor and retrain in production; document trade-offs.

Requirements:
- 4+ years building and deploying ML in production.
- Strong Python; PyTorch, TensorFlow or Hugging Face Transformers.
- Practical NLP (classification, NER, topic modeling, semantic similarity).
- LLM building (OpenAI, Anthropic or open source), prompting, RAG.
- Embeddings and vector search (pgvector, Pinecone, OpenSearch, FAISS).
- Deploy ML services on AWS, GCP or Azure with Docker and CI/CD.
- Model evaluation, error analysis, production monitoring.
- Remote, US, overlap with US Eastern Time; contract; must be authorized to work for any US employer, no visa sponsorship.

Nice to have: trust and safety or threat intelligence; agentic systems (LangChain, LlamaIndex or custom); multilingual NLP; MLflow, SageMaker, Vertex AI, Weights and Biases; Kafka, Spark, Airflow; LoRA, PEFT, distillation.
