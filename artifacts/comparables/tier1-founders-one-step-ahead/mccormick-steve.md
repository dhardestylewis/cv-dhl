# Earth Index (blog, fetched, 2026-04-28 per search metadata)
- Tool for mapping environmental targets using AI foundation models for satellite imagery. Now open to anyone at app.earthindex.ai, free "Open" tier (planet-wide, uploads of reference data, downloads of mapped points); "Full" tier adds higher quotas, Deep Search and API access, free for selected grassroots and high-impact users.
- Launched new embeddings for 2024 and 2025, which Earth Genome says are its most capable per internal evals (internal claim).
- Reported user results: narcotrafficking airstrips in the Peruvian Amazon, illegal palm oil expansion in Brazil, hazardous quarries in the Balkans, rose farming and wetland loss in Uganda.
- Recent posts: "Search for Stuff" natural-language reference layers (2026-09-29), aquaculture regulation validation in Chile (2026-09-22), "Two Foundation Models Are Better Than One: A Second-Stage Classifier on AlphaEarth Embeddings" (2026-09-21).
