# Profile (Yale Clean Energy Forum interview, 2025-04-17)
- Co-founder and Head of Strategy at Grid Status (title is Chief Strategy Officer in 2026 posts).
- Graduated 2011; public policy graduate degree at Indiana; added a second master's in energy systems engineering and pollution; internship at a national lab in California.
- Oak Ridge National Laboratory: group funded by DOE Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy rebuilding US hydropower capacity; learned Python by scripting pulls of every pumped storage permit application to build cost supply curves for NREL baselines.
- Potomac Economics (independent market monitor), energy economics consulting; briefly a virtual power plant startup.
- Black Mountain Energy Storage: Director of Policy and Fundamentals; battery siting and land decisions, market and regulatory analysis. Recruited after publishing a map of IRA "energy communities" built from Bureau of Mines and other federal sources.
- Grid Status started as an open-source project about 2.5 years before the interview (about late 2022); he worked nights and weekends on the energy side while Max Kanter built engineering; nine employees at interview time.
- Works on: nodal price data (ERCOT has almost 18,000 buses; billions of rows), shift factors, interconnection and load growth, ISO stakeholder materials.
- Views stated: load growth projections get "total credulity" and deserve scrutiny of which projects are real, who builds them, and cost allocation; load and generator interconnection are not in sync.
