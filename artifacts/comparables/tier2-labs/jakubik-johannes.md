# Source: IBM Research people page (self-authored bio)

## Bio (condensed from page)
- Research Scientist in the AI for Climate Impact team at IBM Research Europe. Co-leads IBM Research activities on deep learning for planetary observations, especially pretraining and scaling multimodal deep learning models for Earth observation, in collaboration with NASA, ESA and the EU Horizon program. Also contributes to deep learning models for weather and climate assessment.
- Awards named: NASA Agency Group Award, NASA Marshall Space Flight Center Honor Award, several IBM accomplishment awards.
- Mentors PhD students at MIT and ETH Zurich.
- Education: graduated from KIT and ETH (research across data-centric, model-centric and human-centric deep learning). During PhD: a best paper award and a best paper award nomination (human-centric AI theory). Specific degrees and years not stated on the page.
- Fall 2024: recommended as top candidate for a tenure track professorship at a German university of excellence (self-reported).
