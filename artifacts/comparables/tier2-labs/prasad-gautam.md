## PDFM facts (from the abstract on the Mimi Sun page, same paper)
- Graph neural network over US postal codes and counties; inputs: maps, busyness, aggregated search trends, weather, air quality
- Benchmarked on 27 downstream tasks (health, socioeconomic, environmental); SOTA interpolation on all; SOTA extrapolation and super-resolution on 25 of 27
- Combined with TimesFM to forecast unemployment and poverty, reported to beat fully supervised forecasting
- Embeddings and sample code released publicly
