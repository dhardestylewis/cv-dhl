# AlphaEarth Foundations (arXiv:2507.22291, submitted Jul 29, 2025, v2 Sep 8, 2025; 19 authors, Brown first)
- Embedding field model assimilating spatial, temporal and measurement contexts across multiple sources; local to global scales from sparse labels.
- Claim: only embeddings to consistently outperform other tested featurization approaches across diverse mapping evaluations without re-training.
- Released: global annual analysis-ready embedding layers 2017 - 2024.

# DeepMind blog (Jul 30, 2025) facts
- 10 m x 10 m squares; 64-dimensional embeddings; 16x less storage than other tested systems; 24% lower error on average than tested models (self-reported).
- Satellite Embedding dataset in Google Earth Engine, over 1.4 trillion embedding footprints per year; tested with 50+ organizations (FAO, MapBiomas, Harvard Forest, GEO, Stanford and others).
- Team (collaboration of DeepMind and Earth Engine): Christopher Brown, Michal Kazmierski, Valerie Pasquarella, William Rucklidge, Masha Samsikova, Olivia Wiles, Chenhui Zhang, Estefania Lahera, Evan Shelhamer, Simon Ilyushchenko, Noel Gorelick, Lihui Lydia Zhang, Sophia Alj, Emily Schechter, Sean Askay, Oliver Guinan, Rebecca Moore, Alexis Boukouvalas, Pushmeet Kohli.
- IEEE Spectrum: team stresses it is a self-supervised summary framework, not a generative model; stratified sampling used to mitigate training bias.
