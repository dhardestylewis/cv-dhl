## Talk
"Learning Global Weather Forecasting from Data: From Proof of Concept to Operational Deployment". Abstract covers data-driven forecasting breakthroughs, pitfalls of ML in science (validation, community adoption) and next-generation research opportunities.

## Bio (verbatim substance)
- Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, focused on faster and more accurate weather forecasting.
- Methods used: adversarial networks, graph neural networks, diffusion models, functional generative networks. Tools named: precipitation nowcasting (DGMR), global medium-range prediction (GraphCast, GenCast, FGN).
- GraphCast: runner-up for Science's Breakthrough of the Year 2023; received the MacRobert Award of the Royal Academy of Engineering.
- Featured in Nature's 10 (2024 edition).
- PhD, Computational Sciences and Engineering, MIT, advised by Karen Willcox (year not stated).
