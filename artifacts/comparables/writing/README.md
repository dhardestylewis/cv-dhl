# Best-in-class writing on what makes a strong profile, pulled 2026-10-15

77 sources, one file each, fetched by a Sonnet research agent via TinyFish. LinkedIn and Google Scholar were not used. Each file has front matter (author, org, url, date, fetch date, who on the comparables list it connects to, why it matters) and the substantive text, capped near 186 lines. Some files carry mojibake or "[Omitted long matching line]" markers from the fetch tool; they were not cleaned.

## What the best writing says the bar is

1. **Lead with what you can do, not where you learned it.** Anthropic: about half its technical staff had no prior ML experience, about half have PhDs, some never went to college; put independent research, a thoughtful blog post or open-source work at the top of the resume. Fellows: no PhD, prior ML or papers required; about 35% of past fellows joined full-time. OpenAI Residency: self-taught applicants with "a clear record of building and learning with intensity". (anthropic-careers-what-we-are-looking-for, anthropic-fellows-program-2026, openai-residency-page, gordic-how-i-got-a-job-at-deepmind-re)
2. **A public artifact counts when others can run it.** Aurora ships a package, checkpoints and docs; GraphCast, GenCast and AlphaEarth pair result with code or dataset; Keisler's one page plus paper plus code became a widely cited artifact. (microsoft-aurora-docs-intro, deepmind-alphaearth-foundations-blog, keisler-graph-weather-page, yan-why-have-a-data-science-portfolio)
3. **Name the model plainly and give it a family.** EPT-2, GFT, t0-beta, Aurora, TerraMind each carry version, type and parameter count. (jua-ept2-technical-report, silurian-introducing-earth-api-gft, tfc-t0-beta-model-comparison, ibm-terramind-blog)
4. **State scale as parameters, data and inference cost, with a named baseline.** t0-beta 256M next to rivals' sizes; GFT 1.5B; GraphCast under a minute on one TPU v4; GenCast 8 minutes on one TPU v5. (tfc-t0-beta-model-comparison, deepmind-graphcast-blog, deepmind-gencast-blog, balaprakash-track-record-homepage)
5. **A fair comparison fixes the ground truth, the baseline and the data separation.** WeatherBench 2 scores HRES against its own analysis on a common grid; Jua defines three ground-truth sets; GIFT-Eval tags "zero-shot" only with no test leakage. (weatherbench2-data-guide, jua-ept2-technical-report, salesforce-gift-eval-repo)
6. **Pick and defend the metric.** Amperon argues NMAE over MAPE for solar-heavy load; TFC reports CRPS, MASE and win rates and concedes where TimesFM leads; Zillow states a median error and its decline. (amperon-nmae-over-mape, tfc-t0-beta-model-comparison, zillow-building-the-neural-zestimate)
7. **Vendor-run benchmarks are claims, not proof.** Name the baseline, period and metric, and say what the model loses on. (jua-2026-ai-weather-model-benchmarks, silurian-aurora-lights-up-earth-simulations, tfc-t0-beta-model-comparison)
8. **Credible results mean an eval system plus frozen settings.** Unit tests, traces, human or model eval; freeze model, prompts, tools and decoding before comparing. (husain-your-ai-product-needs-evals, raschka-why-llm-evaluation-is-harder)
9. **Labs read a CV for execution evidence, not credentials.** Research engineer postings list a bachelor's or equivalent as the floor; research taste means getting to real experiments early. (anthropic-research-engineer-post-training-job, schulman-opinionated-guide-ml-research, alignmentforum-touch-reality-ml-research)
10. **Profiles that read well are specific and lead with artifacts.** Rothenberg spans academia, Waymo, Pangeo in a few lines; Keisler leads with versatility then paper, code, press; Fainberg gives one line per role. (rothenberg-vita, keisler-personal-site-homepage, fainberg-personal-site-homepage)
11. **Before a seed, show the most impressive thing built, plus validation.** YC: "something impressive each founder has built" is the most important question; include a demo. a16z speedrun: velocity, many customer conversations, an earned insight. Grid Status: open data work nights and weekends, then an $8M round. (yc-how-to-apply, yc-application-tips-include-a-demo, a16z-speedrun-what-we-look-for-in-applications, energize-why-we-invested-in-grid-status)
12. **What SPC selects for:** the minus-one-to-zero stage; about 70% would-be founders, 30% researchers or open-source builders; full-time commitment; show how you generate ideas, including discarded ones; solo founders must build and prototype; $400K for 7% plus $600K in the next round. (spc-faq, spc-founder-fellowship-fall-2026, spc-our-story-minus-one-mindset)
13. **What YC selects for:** clarity first (more than half of interview-worthy groups "blow the application"), then the most impressive specific achievement, determination, and an idea explained concretely. (yc-how-to-apply, graham-what-we-look-for-in-founders, yc-interview-guide)
14. **Written work is a signal in this field.** Explanatory posts and tooling help; the Fellows program expects a research output. (yan-why-have-a-data-science-portfolio, lambert-ai-research-job-market, ghosh-quickml, anthropic-fellows-program-2024-intro)

Caveats recorded by the agent: the Aurora parameter count was not verified from the file; "Pangeo founder" for Rothenberg and "Descartes Labs lineage" for Keisler are labels from the brief, not the sources; Heyman and Fuward are third-party accounts; the Lambert, Yan and Schulman files are truncated.
