## About speedrun

### 

We invest up to $1M in your startup, add $10M+ in credits, and help you with capital, customers, and talent as you build. The in-person part is the residency: twelve weeks of firesides, practitioner sessions, office hours, and events at our San Francisco office, alongside other founders building at the same stage.

### 

Twice a year, both in San Francisco. SR008 starts in February 2027. SR009 starts in late July 2027, with Demo Day on October 5, 2027.

### 

Recent residencies have had 70 to 80 companies. Fewer than 0.4% of applicants are accepted.

### 

Your investing partner and the wider speedrun team, about 40 people. Around eight are investors, and the rest are operators who help with go-to-market, talent, marketing, fundraising, and more.

### 

We invest in founders across almost every sector, from AI and robotics to fintech, consumer, B2B, security, and more. What matters most to us is the team, not the category. If you’re building something you believe in, we’d love for you to apply.

### 

The alpha fellowship invests up to $250K plus credits in students, recent grads, and dropouts who are still finding their idea, product, or team. speedrun invests up to $1M in founders who are already building, or further along in their careers, with an idea, a product, and a team.

### 

Yes. You'll have an investing partner who knows your company, plus a team of about 40 people focused on helping companies at your stage with capital, customers, and talent.
