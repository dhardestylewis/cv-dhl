I lead new research and development for Zillow’s Zestimate
and related home valuation products. Prior to Zillow I taught research
methods and data science to real estate students at the University of
Melbourne and worked as a data scientist developing Automated Valuation
Models (AVMs) for Greenfield Advisors, a valuation consulting firm. My
doctoral degree is in Urban Planning from the University of Washington
and I try to remain active in academic conferences and publications. My
primary research and professional aim is to straddle the academic and
applied sides of the valuation industry. When not writing R code or
staring at plots, I can be found hiking, biking and paddling with my
family in the Pacific Northwest.
