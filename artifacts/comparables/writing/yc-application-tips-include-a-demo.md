# YC Application Tips: Include a demo

by Stephanie Simon

59K views

Over 1 year ago

YC’s Stephanie Simon on why you should include a demo  -  even a rough one!  -  when you apply to Y Combinator.

## Transcript

One thing that I wish more founders would include on their application is a demo of their product. So many applications we get or ideas, someone's been thinking about for a year with nothing done. We don't care if the demo is unpolished. We don't care if you're still running it locally or if it's just a CSV right now. The difference between nothing and something is actually huge to us. It shows us that you're serious and that you can get something built.
